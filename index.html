01

About FaceForge

Isman App Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates FaceForge, an Android app offering AI-assisted face and grooming observations, hairstyle inspiration, personal wardrobe organization, outfit planning, and related style tools. Our official website is faceforge.org.

This page describes our information practices. AI outputs—including face scores, skin observations, advice, and generated hairstyle images—are illustrative or informational, may be inaccurate, and are not a medical diagnosis or a guarantee of personal appearance outcomes.

Important distinction: Some tools use AI; others rely on your saved clothing, local image checks, existing catalog information, or deterministic selection. Browsing a hairstyle catalog does not itself mean a new AI analysis is performed.

02

What the app does

These are the main feature areas represented in the current FaceForge product. Actual availability may depend on your installed version, account, plan, and the current service configuration.

AI Face Scan & Coach

Submit a portrait for AI-assisted observations, view analysis history, and ask for grooming or routine suggestions.

Hairstyle Studio

Explore a curated library of hairstyle ideas, capture or select a portrait, and request AI previews for supported styles.

Digital Wardrobe

Save clothing items in your own categories, manage your wardrobe, and request optional AI-assisted classification.

Daily Outfits & Shopping

Find combinations from your stored clothing, save outfit ideas, and request AI styling or shopping analysis where available.

Routines & Progress

Use guided face exercises, self-care plans, reminders, streaks, and progress or saved-history views.

Saved Results

Reopen eligible face scans, hairstyles, shopping analyses, plans, and outfits associated with your account.

The hairstyle catalog includes 65 curated reference entries. AI hairstyle image previews are limited to styles currently marked as preview-supported; a catalog reference is not a promise of a working AI preview.

  • Provide selected face, hairstyle, wardrobe, shopping, outfit, and AI coaching services.
  • Store requested analysis results, saved images, plans, and history for later reopening.

We do not treat AI-generated face scores as objective assessments of attractiveness, health, or identity.

06

How AI processes your content

When you request an AI-dependent feature, relevant prompts, selected photos, item images, or profile context may be sent from our backend to a third-party AI provider such as the Google Gemini API. The information transmitted depends on the specific operation; simple browsing, cached history, and certain outfit suggestions do not require a new generative-AI request.

What AI results mean

AI suggestions can contain errors, incomplete observations, unusual images, or unsuitable styling advice. Hairstyle previews are illustrations, not guaranteed salon outcomes. Skin-related observations and exercise guidance are not clinical or dermatological advice.

We keep relevant results or input media when necessary to support account history or an active operation, subject to deletion controls. We do not claim that AI providers retain no information; provider data handling is subject to their applicable service terms and settings.

08

Security, storage & retention

FaceForge uses authenticated access and owner-scoped controls for stored user content, private media buckets, temporary signed links for protected images, and HTTPS for supported network transmission. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

How long data is kept

  • Face, hair, wardrobe, and shopping media: may remain in private account storage for active operations and saved history until removal or a valid deletion request. We do not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period.
  • Saved results, plans, messages, and outfits: may remain associated with your account until you remove eligible content or request applicable deletion.
  • Account/profile records: remain while an account exists, subject to account-deletion processing.
  • Transactions and service integrity records: certain records may be retained after content deletion or account closure when required for payments, fraud prevention, security, or legal obligations.
  • Backups, provider logs, and diagnostics: may follow relevant operational or provider retention schedules; specific periods can vary.

Correction to our prior policy: Earlier versions said face images were automatically removed after 30 days. That statement is no longer made because automatic cleanup cannot currently be assured.

09

Delete your data or account

FaceForge provides separate types of deletion. They do not have the same effect.

Delete My Data

The in-app Delete My Data control requests removal of supported saved content such as scans, plans, coach history, hairstyle media, wardrobe images, shopping content, and other owned feature records. It does not by itself close your login account, cancel subscriptions, or necessarily erase purchase/security records.

Delete Account

A full account-deletion request is for your FaceForge authentication identity and associated account data, with eligible owned content removed. Some payment, fraud-prevention, security, or legally required records may need limited retention.

Request account deletion without installing the app

If you want to delete your FaceForge account, email ismanappstudio@gmail.com with the subject FaceForge Account Deletion Request. You may use this option even if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in. Please say that you are requesting permanent deletion of your FaceForge account and associated data.

We may ask you to verify account ownership securely before processing the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, purchase tokens, identity documents, or personal photos in your first email. We handle requests in accordance with applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

This section is an off-app request method, not an automated online deletion backend. We must verify and process valid requests; the button opens the user's email app and does not itself delete an account.

Eligible Free experiences may show standard advertising or offer a voluntary rewarded ad. Paid entitlements may suppress standard ads where the app's verified plan rules apply. 11

Why AI-powered services use resources

Some FaceForge features require substantially more processing than a standard information screen. This helps explain why certain features have usage allowances, subscriptions, credits, or optional ad-supported access.

AI analysis & reasoning

Selected photos, long conversations, wardrobe classification, shopping analysis, and advanced styling can require provider processing and sometimes several AI steps.

Image generation

Creating an illustrated hairstyle preview or other generated visual is more resource-intensive than simply reopening a previously saved image.

Private storage & delivery

Keeping portraits, wardrobe photos, saved results, and histories available requires secure storage, database operations, and network transfer.

Service reliability

Account security, purchase verification, abuse controls, notifications, error monitoring, backend execution, and recovery processes also consume infrastructure resources.

This is a general operational explanation, not a statement that every interaction generates a paid AI request. Browsing existing styles and reopening saved results may avoid new generative-AI processing.
